# Distribution Plan (Phase 7)

Distribution is a business activity, not a code deliverable — nothing in this file is something a build session can execute on your behalf. It's a working plan and set of templates for the actual outreach, written from PLAN.md's channel strategy so you're not starting from a blank page.

## 1. Adviser pilot (do this first)

PLAN.md's own honest-expectations section is blunt: the build only really "starts" once an adviser firm agrees to pilot it. If Phase 0's interviews (see STATUS.md) haven't happened yet, that's the actual next step — not more building.

**Target**: 2–3 R&D tax consultancies or accounting firms with an R&D practice, willing to pilot free with 1–2 of their clients in exchange for shaping the product and a reference.

**Outreach email template:**

> Subject: A way to make your R&D claims defend themselves at enquiry
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm building a tool that captures R&D evidence contemporaneously through the year — time apportionment and the technical narrative, written down as the work happens rather than reconstructed at claim time. It connects to Xero for the cost side and produces a claim-ready evidence pack per project, per period.
>
> I'm not trying to replace your claim-prep work — the opposite: better contemporaneous evidence makes the claims you prepare more defensible and faster to put together, since the narrative and the time apportionment basis are already sitting there instead of being reconstructed from memory in month 11.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute call? I'd like to understand how your clients currently evidence time apportionment, and what happens when HMRC opens an enquiry — and if it looks like a fit, I'd love to pilot this free with one or two of your clients.
>
> [name]

**What "shaping the product" should mean in practice**: a standing fortnightly 15-minute check-in during the pilot, direct access to raise friction points, and first look at the evidence pack template before it's finalised for anyone else.

## 2. Content (needs a real, credentialed author — not this session)

PLAN.md is explicit: "an accountant explaining what HMRC actually asks for at enquiry and what evidence answers it... the AI-generated competition cannot write this credibly." That's not a task to delegate to a generation pass — the credibility *is* the content. What's provided here is a brief, not a draft, so whoever writes it (you, or a bought-in accountant/adviser) isn't starting from nothing.

**Article brief: "What HMRC actually asks for at enquiry"**
- Open with a real (anonymised) enquiry request list — the actual documents/answers HMRC asked a claimant to produce.
- For each item on that list, name what evidence answers it, and what "answering it well" vs. "answering it badly six months late" looks like.
- Close on the two weakest links PLAN.md names — staff time apportionment and the technical narrative — and why evidence written *at the time* beats evidence reconstructed later, with a concrete example if you have one.
- Length: 800–1,200 words. Publish under a named, credentialed byline — anonymous or clearly AI-voiced content undermines the exact credibility this piece is supposed to establish.

**Where this lives once written**: a blog/resources section on the marketing site (currently just the Next.js starter page at `app/page.tsx` — replacing it with a real marketing page is a separate, smaller piece of work worth doing before this content has anywhere to land).

## 3. The evidence pack as referral loop

No action needed beyond what's already built (Phase 5) — the PDF pack generated by `/api/exports/[projectId]/[accountingPeriodId]` is the artifact that lands on an adviser's desk with the product's name on it. Worth deciding before the first pilot: what the pack's cover page/branding should say, and whether a white-label option (adviser's branding instead) is offered from day one or added after the first few pilots validate demand for it.

## 4. Xero App Store / App Partner listing

Second channel, after reliability and reviews are solid — not a launch-day priority. When ready:
- Xero App Store listing requires passing Xero's app review (security questionnaire, OAuth flow review, support contact). The OAuth implementation in `lib/xero/oauth.ts` already requests only read scopes, which simplifies that review.
- A Xero App Partner listing is a separate application process on top of the App Store listing, and is the better long-term channel given how much of this ecosystem's tools grow through accountant referral (PLAN.md notes this explicitly).
- Both require a live, production Xero OAuth app registration (Client ID/Secret) — see STATUS.md for what's still a placeholder.

## Sequencing

1. Adviser interviews (Phase 0, if not already done) → pilot commitment from at least one firm.
2. Pilot with that firm's client(s) using what's built here.
3. Content piece, written with a real byline, once there's at least one real (anonymised) story to draw from.
4. Xero listings, once the pilot has produced reviews/reliability data worth pointing to.
